# Visuals restored from the original lesson

These assets support the functions, functionals, and Palmer Penguins sequence
restored from this repository's pre-2026 presentation. Slide-level source notes,
links, captions, and alt text are also present in `Presentation/presentation.qmd`.

## Advanced R diagrams

`advanced-r-fp.png` is the functional-programming input/output matrix from the
[Advanced R functional-programming introduction](https://adv-r.hadley.nz/fp.html).
`advanced-r-map-list.png` is the list-mapping diagram from the
[Functionals chapter](https://adv-r.hadley.nz/functionals.html).

Advanced R is written by Hadley Wickham and licensed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://adv-r.hadley.nz/).

## Palmer Penguins artwork

`palmerpenguins-art.png`, `penguin-anatomy.png`, and `lter-penguins.png` come
from the [`palmerpenguins` documentation](https://allisonhorst.github.io/palmerpenguins/).
The artwork is by Allison Horst and the package distributes it under
[CC0](https://allisonhorst.github.io/palmerpenguins/LICENSE.html).

The assets introduce the three penguin species and the bill measurements used
in the progressively constructed functional workflow.
